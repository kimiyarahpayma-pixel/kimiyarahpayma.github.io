ABOUT

I am an artist and sculptor working across sculpture, functional objects, and visual narratives.

My practice explores the body, intimacy, transformation, and the strange feeling of carrying a home within oneself.

Through surreal forms, tactile materials, and recurring symbols such as the spiral and the shell, I build characters and objects that exist somewhere between the familiar and the uncanny.
