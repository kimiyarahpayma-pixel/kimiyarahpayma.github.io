ABOUT ME

Things I couldn’t say became things I could touch.

They began as sensations—something under the skin, a thought that wouldn’t leave, a desire without a name. Slowly, they grew bodies of their own. Flesh, shells, mouths, folds, spirals. I started building them, almost as if I were meeting parts of myself I had kept hidden.

My sculptures live somewhere between the body and the object, the tender and the grotesque, intimacy and defense. I am drawn to the strange moment when something soft begins to grow a shell, when something wounded becomes beautiful, when the things we hide begin asking to be seen.

I make these objects to give shape to what lingers inside me—to make the invisible touchable, and to see what happens when I finally let it exist outside my body.
